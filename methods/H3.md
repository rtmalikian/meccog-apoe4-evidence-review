# Method — H3 (APOE4 → tau pathology)

Search: Europe PMC, two runs (see search-logs/2026-11-18.md and 2026-11-18-run2.md). Run 1: 7,863 hits, top 200 by citations screened (only 3 eligible — the pool was 84% reviews). Run 2 (reviews excluded at source): 3,492 hits, top 200 screened, deduplicated against Run 1.

Eligibility: experimental studies directly testing APOE4 (vs APOE3/APOE2, or APOE4 manipulation) effects on tau phosphorylation, release, propagation/spread, tau pathology burden, or tau-related neuroinflammation.

Screening: dual (Screener A inclusion, independent Screener B verification). Result: 11 confirmed papers, 24 findings extracted. Held out as disputed pending full-text adjudication: Shi 2019, Yin 2023, Andrews-Zwilling 2010, Chang 2005, Strittmatter 1994 (reasons in screening/H3-verified.md). Four papers were abstract-only; their unverifiable statistics carry full_text_unavailable provenance.
