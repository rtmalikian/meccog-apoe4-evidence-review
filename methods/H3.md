# H3 — Method note (official hypothesis, corrected run 2026-11-18)

**Hypothesis (verbatim):** "In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, APOE4 undergoes increased aggregation in the endo-lysosome compared with APOE3."

**Searches (Europe PMC, resultType=core, citation-sorted):** Run 1: (APOE4 OR apoE4 OR "apolipoprotein E4") AND aggregat* AND (endolysosom* OR "endo-lysosom*" OR lysosom* OR endosom*) — 3,267 hits. Run 2 (reviews excluded): 1,025 hits. 370 deduped records screened. A supplementary looser search ((APOE terms) AND (aggregation OR aggregates OR aggregated), 9,181 hits, top 100 screened) found no new candidates.

**Eligibility:** experimental studies jointly establishing (a) aggregation of apoE itself, (b) an APOE4-vs-APOE3/APOE2 comparison or APOE4 manipulation, and (c) endo-lysosomal localization of the aggregates.

**Screening (dual, independent) + full-text adjudication:** dual screening confirmed ZERO papers; three were held out as disputed (Rawat 2019, Zhao 2017, Kaji 2024) and went to full-text adjudication. Rawat and Zhao both measure apoE aggregation with isoform comparisons but localize nothing — their aggregates are whole-cell/cortical fractions, so both remain held out on element (c). Kaji 2024 (the challenge's starting reference; absent from both search runs — a retrieval gap, since its abstract carries neither the search's aggregation-plus-endolysosome term pairing nor any isoform name) was CONFIRMED on its full text: APOE4-specific aggregate formation in cell-free and knockin arms, and APOE aggregation inside the microglial endo-lysosomal system. The caveat is recorded in the extraction: the isoform contrast and the localization come from different experimental arms, and the knockin localization model carries mouse APOE.

**Result:** 1 paper, 3 findings. This is the honest yield for a narrowly worded hypothesis; the near-miss papers and the reasons they fail are documented in screening/H3-verified.md.
