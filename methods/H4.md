# H4 — Method note (official hypothesis, corrected run 2026-11-18)

**Hypothesis (verbatim):** "In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, APOE4 adheres more tightly to some recycling receptors than APOE3, impeding recycling."

**Searches (Europe PMC, resultType=core, citation-sorted):** Run 1: (APOE4 OR apoE4 OR "apolipoprotein E4") AND recycl* AND (receptor* OR retromer OR sortilin OR ApoER2 OR LRP1 OR VPS35) — 1,736 hits. Run 2 (reviews excluded): 572 hits. 366 deduped records screened.

**Eligibility:** experimental studies directly testing APOE4 vs APOE3/APOE2 binding/adhesion to recycling receptors with the recycling consequence measured or directly assayed.

**Screening (dual, independent):** Screener A shortlisted 8; Screener B verified 5 confirmed, 3 disputed held out (Chen 2010, Prasad & Rao 2018, Altenburg 2008 — recycling or binding measured, but the binding-to-impeded-recycling link not jointly established).

**Result:** 5 papers, 12 findings, including 1 contradicting finding (Xian 2018's own binding data: APOE3 and APOE4 bound ApoER2 with similar affinity — the delayed-dissociation mechanism is Pohlkamp 2021's). Xian 2018 (the challenge's starting reference) confirmed and ranked first. Guo 2025's E3-grouped-with-E4 caveat is stated in its finding. No abstract-only papers.
