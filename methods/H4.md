# Method — H4 (APOE4 → lipid accumulation and cell stress)

Search: Europe PMC, two runs (see search-logs/2026-11-18.md and 2026-11-18-run2.md). Run 1: 2,802 hits, top 200 by citations screened. Run 2 (reviews excluded at source): 960 hits, top 200 screened, deduplicated against Run 1.

Eligibility: experimental studies directly testing APOE4 (vs APOE3/APOE2, or APOE4 manipulation) effects on lipid accumulation/droplets, lipid transport or turnover, or lysosomal function in astrocytes, microglia, or neurons, including resulting cell stress/dysfunction.

Screening: dual (Screener A inclusion, independent Screener B verification) plus adjudication of 13 borderline cases (1 INCLUDE: Rawat 2019, which is also an H1 paper — a cross-hypothesis link). Result: 17 papers, 33 findings extracted. Verification caught two record errors, corrected before extraction: the Huang organoid paper is 10.1038/s41392-022-01006-x (not the 10.1126/sciadv.ade0764 recorded at first pass, which is an unrelated paper), and Farmer 2019 is 10.3390/cells8020182. One paper (Rawat 2019) was abstract-only in practice (PMC record carried front matter only).
