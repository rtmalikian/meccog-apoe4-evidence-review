# Method — H1 (APOE4 → ABCA1 lipid transport)

Search: Europe PMC, two runs (see search-logs/2026-11-18.md and 2026-11-18-run2.md). Run 1: 1,055 hits, top 200 by citations screened. Run 2 (reviews excluded at source): 417 hits, top 200 screened, deduplicated against Run 1.

Eligibility: experimental studies directly testing APOE4 (vs APOE3/APOE2, or APOE4 manipulation) effects on ABCA1-mediated lipid transport / cholesterol efflux, or lipid availability at the blood-brain or blood-CSF barrier. Reviews, GWAS-only, and non-mechanistic clinical/epidemiological studies excluded.

Screening: dual (Screener A inclusion, independent Screener B verification against the abstracts). Result: 14 confirmed papers, 28 findings extracted. Held out as disputed pending full-text adjudication: Fitz 2012 (no lipid-transport endpoint measured), Moulton 2021 (no APOE4-vs-APOE3/APOE2 comparison in the abstract). One included paper (Lindner 2022) was abstract-only; its unverifiable statistics carry full_text_unavailable provenance. A contradicting finding (ABCA1-mediated HDL-like particle formation largely unaffected by isoform) is recorded as evidence, per the challenge rules.
