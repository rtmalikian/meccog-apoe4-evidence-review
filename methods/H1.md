# H1 — Method note (official hypothesis, corrected run 2026-11-18)

**Hypothesis (verbatim):** "In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, for example astrocytes, APOE4 causes reduced endosome pH compared with APOE3."

**Searches (Europe PMC, resultType=core, citation-sorted):** Run 1: (APOE4 OR apoE4 OR "apolipoprotein E4") AND endosom* AND (pH OR acidification OR acidified OR acidify) — 977 hits. Run 2: same string excluding reviews/syntheses/editorials — 471 hits. Top 200 of each screened; 322 deduped records.

**Eligibility:** experimental studies directly testing APOE4 vs APOE3/APOE2 (or APOE4 manipulation) on endosomal/endosome pH or acidification in brain cells; model systems count per the challenge guidance. Reviews, GWAS-only, clinical correlation-only, editorials and keyword-only matches excluded.

**Screening (dual, independent):** Screener A shortlisted 3; Screener B verified 2 confirmed, 1 disputed held out (Pohlkamp 2021 — acidification manipulated as a rescue but endosomal pH never compared by isoform).

**Result:** 2 papers, 5 findings. Prasad & Rao 2018 (the challenge's starting reference) confirmed and ranked first — the pool's only direct isoform comparison of endosomal pH (0.84 pH units lower in APOE4 astrocytes). Xian 2018 confirmed on its acidification-manipulation arm only, directness class 4 / weak, with its directional tension against Prasad stated in the finding text. No abstract-only papers.
