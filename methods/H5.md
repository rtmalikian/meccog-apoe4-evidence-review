# H5 — Method note (official hypothesis, corrected run 2026-11-18)

**Hypothesis (verbatim):** "In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, APOE4 disruption of lipid metabolism, compared with APOE3, results in impaired late endosome/lysosome function."

**Searches (Europe PMC, resultType=core, citation-sorted):** Run 1: (APOE4 OR apoE4 OR "apolipoprotein E4") AND (cholesterol OR "lipid metabolism") AND (lysosom* OR "late endosome" OR endolysosom*) — 2,442 hits. Run 2 (reviews excluded): 800 hits. 363 deduped records screened.

**Eligibility:** experimental studies directly testing APOE4 vs APOE3/APOE2 (or APOE4 manipulation) where lipid-metabolism disruption is linked to late endosome/lysosome dysfunction.

**Screening (dual, independent):** Screener A shortlisted 11; Screener B verified 8 confirmed, 4 disputed held out (Cao 2021, Wang C 2023, Wang ZH 2021, Machlovi 2022). Two confirmations (Tcw 2022, de Leeuw 2022) rest on the lysosomal cholesterol sequestration/egress arm only and are scored down on directness accordingly.

**Result:** 8 papers, 18 findings. Lee 2023 (the challenge's starting reference) confirmed and ranked first. Two papers are abstract-only (Lee 2023 — no PMCID; Rawat 2019 — publisher does not release the full text; its abstract carries the key result verbatim, and unverifiable statistics are recorded "N/A" with full_text_unavailable provenance).
