# Method — H5 (APOE4 → synaptic and neuronal dysfunction)

Search: Europe PMC, two runs (see search-logs/2026-11-18.md and 2026-11-18-run2.md). Run 1: 5,390 hits, top 200 by citations screened. Run 2 (reviews excluded at source): 2,145 hits, top 200 screened, deduplicated against Run 1.

Eligibility: experimental studies directly testing APOE4 (vs APOE3/APOE2, or APOE4 manipulation) effects on synaptic function/structure, neuronal calcium handling, synaptic gene expression, neural network activity, or neuronal resilience.

Screening: dual (Screener A inclusion, independent Screener B verification) plus adjudication of 8 borderline cases (2 INCLUDE: Meyer 2019; Zhao N 2020). Result: 16 papers, 33 findings extracted. All 33 quotes were verified verbatim programmatically against the source texts. Direction is reported as found, not as hoped: Zhao 2020 organoids showed no significant APOE4 effect on synaptophysin/PSD95 (recorded CONTRADICTING), and three papers found APOE4-associated increases in synapse formation or synaptic gene expression, recorded as dysregulation with the direction stated. Five papers were abstract-only.
