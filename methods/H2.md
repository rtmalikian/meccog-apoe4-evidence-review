# H2 — Method note (official hypothesis, corrected run 2026-11-18)

**Hypothesis (verbatim):** "In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, for example astrocytes, APOE4 causes impaired ARF6-related recycling of receptors between the cell surface and the endosome, compared with APOE3."

**Searches (Europe PMC, resultType=core, citation-sorted):** Run 1: (APOE4 OR apoE4 OR "apolipoprotein E4") AND (ARF6 OR "receptor recycling" OR "endosomal recycling" OR "recycling endosome" OR "endocytic recycling") — 300 hits. Run 2 (reviews excluded): 130 hits — the whole non-review pool was screened. 253 deduped records.

**Eligibility:** experimental studies directly testing APOE4 vs APOE3/APOE2 (or APOE4 manipulation) on ARF6-pathway recycling of receptors between the cell surface and the endosome.

**Screening (dual, independent):** Screener A shortlisted 7; Screener B verified 4 confirmed, 3 disputed held out (Prasad 2018, Zhao 2017, Safieh 2024 — endosomal trapping/surface distribution measured but no recycling assay; genuinely arguable both ways, so held out rather than forced).

**Result:** 4 papers, 12 findings, including 1 contradicting finding (Guo 2025: no APOE4-vs-APOE3 difference in LDLR recycling — the defect groups E3 with E4 against E2). Rawat 2019 (the challenge's starting reference) confirmed and ranked first — the only pool record naming ARF6. No abstract-only papers.
