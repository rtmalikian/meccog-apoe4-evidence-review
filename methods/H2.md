# Method — H2 (APOE4 → amyloid β load)

Search: Europe PMC, two runs (see search-logs/2026-11-18.md and 2026-11-18-run2.md). Run 1: 7,760 hits, top 200 by citations screened. Run 2 (reviews excluded at source): 3,928 hits, top 200 screened, deduplicated against Run 1.

Eligibility: experimental studies directly testing APOE4 (vs APOE3/APOE2, or APOE4 manipulation) effects on amyloid β production, clearance, aggregation/load, or amyloid-driven inflammation. Reviews, GWAS-only, and correlation-only clinical/imaging studies excluded.

Screening: dual (Screener A inclusion, independent Screener B verification). Result: 24 confirmed papers, 34 findings extracted, including three documented contradicting/null findings (no isoform effect on Aβ synthesis, Castellano 2011; no production effect in mouse neurons, Wang 2018; no plaque-load change with late apoE lowering, Huynh 2017). Held out as disputed: Haney 2024 and Lee 2023 (Aβ not a measured outcome on verification). Adjudicated EXCLUDE at screening: Shibata 2000, Cramer 2012, Bales 1999 (apoE-dependent but not APOE4-isoform-specific). Six papers were abstract-only (1993-2000 in-vitro studies whose accessible text is abstract + references). Wisniewski 1994 (PMID 7977635) has no DOI in Europe PMC, PubMed, or Crossref-verifiable form; its DOI cell is recorded as N/A rather than guessed.
