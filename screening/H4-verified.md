# H4 Verification — Screener B (independent second screen)

**Hypothesis H4 (verbatim):** "APOE4 causes lipid accumulation in astrocytes, microglia, and neurons, through decreased lipid transport, reduced lipid turnover, and lysosomal dysfunction, producing cell stress and cell dysfunction, which then increases disease risk."

**Inclusion criterion applied:** experimental study directly testing APOE4 (vs APOE3/APOE2, or APOE4 manipulation) effects on lipid accumulation/droplets, lipid transport or turnover, or lysosomal function, in astrocytes, microglia, or neurons. Every paper below was checked against its abstract as recorded in `work/searches/H4.json` / `work/searches/H4r2.json`.

**Summary:** 16 Screener A inclusions reviewed → 15 confirmed (1 with a DOI correction), 1 disputed as recorded (DOI error; the intended paper qualifies under its correct DOI). 13 borderline cases adjudicated → 1 INCLUDE, 12 EXCLUDE.

## 1. Confirmed inclusions (15)

1. **DOI 10.1038/s41586-024-07185-7** — 2024 — Haney MS — "APOE4/4 is linked to damaging lipid droplets in Alzheimer's disease microglia."
   - Verified: ACSL1-positive lipid-droplet microglial state most abundant in APOE4/4 AD brain; in human iPSC-derived microglia, fibrillar Aβ induced triglyceride synthesis and lipid droplet accumulation in an APOE-dependent manner.
2. **DOI 10.1126/scitranslmed.aaz4564** — 2021 — Sienski G — "APOE4 disrupts intracellular lipid homeostasis in human iPSC-derived glia."
   - Verified: APOE4, but not APOE3, disrupted lipidomes of human iPSC-derived astrocytes, increasing fatty acid unsaturation and intracellular lipid droplet accumulation.
3. **DOI 10.1016/j.stem.2022.07.005** — 2022 — Victor MB — "Lipid accumulation induced by APOE4 impairs microglial surveillance of neuronal-network activity."
   - Verified: CRISPR-edited iPSC co-cultures; APOE4 induced a lipid-accumulated state in microglia vs APOE3, with decreased uptake of extracellular fatty acids and lipoproteins.
4. **DOI 10.1016/j.celrep.2020.108572** — 2021 — Qi G — "ApoE4 Impairs Neuron-Astrocyte Coupling of Fatty Acid Metabolism."
   - Verified: ApoE4 decreased fatty acid sequestration in neuronal lipid droplets, diminished neuron-to-astrocyte fatty acid transport, lowered fatty acid oxidation, and led to lipid accumulation in astrocytes and hippocampus.
5. **DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2022.05.017** — 2022 — Tcw J — "Cholesterol and matrisome pathways dysregulated in astrocytes and microglia."
   - Verified: APOE4 drove lipid metabolic dysregulation in astrocytes and microglia; APOE4 astrocytes showed elevated intracellular cholesterol due to lysosomal cholesterol sequestration.
6. **DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2018.05.008** — 2018 — Lin YT — "APOE4 Causes Widespread Molecular and Cellular Alterations Associated with Alzheimer's Disease Phenotypes in Human iPSC-Derived Brain Cell Types."
   - Verified: isogenic iPSC brain cell types; lipid metabolism was the most affected pathway in APOE4 astrocytes, which displayed cholesterol accumulation relative to APOE3.
7. **DOI 10.1016/j.nbd.2022.105615** — 2022 — Machlovi SI — "APOE4 confers transcriptomic and functional alterations to primary mouse microglia."
   - Verified: primary microglia from humanized APOE4 mice showed, vs APOE3, increased endolysosomal mass and increased lipid and lipid droplet accumulation at baseline, with integrated-stress-response activation.
8. **DOI 10.1038/s41590-023-01640-9** — 2023 — Liu CC — "Cell-autonomous effects of APOE4 in restricting microglial response in brain homeostasis and Alzheimer's disease."
   - Verified: conditional mouse models; microglial apoE4 (unlike apoE3) impaired lipid metabolism and downregulated lysosomal pathways; increased lipid droplet accumulation in APOE4 human brains and iPSC-derived microglia.
9. **DOI 10.1083/jcb.202305003** — 2024 — Windham IA — "APOE traffics to astrocyte lipid droplets and modulates triglyceride saturation and droplet size."
   - Verified: APOE4-expressing astrocytes formed few large lipid droplets enriched in unsaturated triglyceride, with impaired droplet turnover and increased sensitivity to lipid peroxidation.
10. **DOI 10.1016/j.neuron.2023.10.023** — 2024 — Litvinchuk A — "Amelioration of Tau and ApoE4-linked glial lipid accumulation and neurodegeneration with an LXR agonist."
    - Verified: in P301S tau mice, ApoE4 (vs ApoE3 or no ApoE) strongly promoted glial lipid accumulation with perturbed cholesterol metabolism and lysosomal function.
11. **DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2024.10.027** — 2025 — Guo JL — "Decreased lipidated ApoE-receptor interactions confer protection against pathogenicity of ApoE and its lipid cargoes in lysosomes."
    - Verified: in human neurons, ApoE carrying PUFA-cholesteryl esters produced an allelic series (ApoE4 > ApoE3 > ApoE2) for lipofuscinosis, a lysosomal lipid-peroxidation pathology; lipofuscin elevated in APOE4 mouse brain.
12. **DOI 10.1016/j.stemcr.2021.11.007** — 2022 — de Leeuw SM — "APOE2, E3, and E4 differentially modulate cellular homeostasis, cholesterol metabolism, and inflammatory response in isogenic iPSC-derived astrocytes."
    - Verified: isogenic iPSC-derived astrocytes; APOE4 reduced cholesterol efflux and biosynthesis while lysosomal localization of cholesterol was elevated, genotype-graded (E4 > E3 > E2).
13. **DOI 10.1038/s12276-023-00935-z** — 2023 — Wang C — "TRPV1 regulates ApoE4-disrupted intracellular lipid homeostasis and decreases synaptic phagocytosis by microglia."
    - Verified: ApoE4 disrupted intracellular lipid homeostasis in microglia; lipid accumulation accelerated in TRPV1-deficient ApoE4 microglia and rescued by TRPV1 activation in ApoE4 microglia and neurons.
14. **DOI 10.1186/s13024-022-00577-1** — 2022 — Wang N — "Opposing effects of apoE2 and apoE4 on microglial activation and lipid metabolism in response to demyelination."
    - Verified: apoE targeted-replacement mice under cuprizone demyelination; apoE4 microglia cleared lipid-rich myelin debris less efficiently than apoE3 and showed excessive lipid droplet buildup.
15. **DOI 10.3390/cells8020182** — 2019 — Farmer BC — "Apolipoprotein E4 Alters Astrocyte Fatty Acid Metabolism and Lipid Droplet Formation." **[DOI corrected — see note]**
    - Verified on content: E4 astrocytes accumulated significantly more and smaller lipid droplets than E3 astrocytes, with decreased fatty acid uptake, decreased oxidation of exogenous oleate/palmitate, and accumulation of lipid-droplet-derived metabolites.
    - **Note:** Screener A recorded this paper as DOI 10.3389/fncel.2019.00182, which does not appear in either search pool. The matching record in `H4r2.json` (same title, authors Farmer BC, Kluemper J, Johnson LA) carries DOI 10.3390/cells8020182 (Cells, 2019). The DOI in the shortlist should be corrected; the paper itself meets the criterion.

## 2. Disputed inclusions (1)

- **Huang S 2022, as recorded under DOI 10.1126/sciadv.ade0764** — "Chimeric cerebral organoids reveal the essentials of neuronal and astrocytic APOE4 for Alzheimer's tau pathology."
  - **Reason:** DOI mismatch. The abstract on record in `H4r2.json` for DOI 10.1126/sciadv.ade0764 is a different paper entirely (Yang H et al., "Mechanistic insight into female predominance in Alzheimer's disease based on aberrant protein S-nitrosylation of C3"), which contains no APOE4 experiment and no lipid endpoint and does **not** meet the criterion. The intended organoid paper is present in the same pool under DOI **10.1038/s41392-022-01006-x**, and its abstract **does** meet the criterion (in chimeric human cerebral organoids, astrocytic APOE4 significantly promoted lipid droplet formation and cholesterol accumulation in neurons, maximal when neuronal APOE4 was also present). Recommendation: correct the DOI to 10.1038/s41392-022-01006-x and retain the paper; do not submit it under the recorded DOI.

## 3. Borderline adjudications (13)

1. **Blanchard JW 2022 (10.1038/s41586-022-05439-w) — EXCLUDE.** APOE4-driven cholesterol accumulation is demonstrated experimentally, but in oligodendrocytes, which are outside H4's named cell types (astrocytes, microglia, neurons).
2. **Liu L 2017 (10.1016/j.cmet.2017.08.024) — EXCLUDE.** Drosophila glia-neuron system using the fly APOE ortholog; although the abstract states human APOE4 is impaired in lipid transport in this system, no APOE4-vs-APOE3 comparison of lipid accumulation in astrocytes, microglia, or neurons is reported.
3. **Mi Y 2023 (10.1038/s42255-023-00756-4) — EXCLUDE.** Loss of astrocytic mitochondrial fatty acid degradation causes lipid droplet accumulation, but the abstract reports no APOE4 comparison or manipulation.
4. **Lee JH 2022 (10.1038/s41593-022-01084-8) — EXCLUDE.** Faulty autolysosome acidification in AD mouse model neurons, but no APOE4 test, and the measured endpoint is Aβ build-up, not lipid.
5. **Jiang Q 2008 (10.1016/j.neuron.2008.04.010) — EXCLUDE.** ApoE isoform-dependent lysosomal (endolytic) degradation measured in microglia, but the substrate was Aβ; intracellular lipid handling was not measured.
6. **Rawat V 2019 (10.1523/jneurosci.1400-19.2019) — INCLUDE.** Direct APOE4-vs-APOE3 test in astrocytes: ApoE4 trapped ABCA1 in late endosomes and lowered ABCA1-mediated cholesterol efflux — a direct experimental test of the decreased lipid transport limb of H4, with an endolysosomal mechanism, even though intracellular accumulation itself was not the endpoint.
7. **Lindner K 2022 (10.1016/j.celrep.2022.110435) — EXCLUDE.** Isoform-specific ApoE lipidation in astrocytes, but the endpoint is assembly/secretion of triacylglycerol-rich lipoproteins (extracellular particle lipidation), not intracellular lipid accumulation, turnover, or lysosomal function.
8. **Nuriel T 2017 (10.3389/fnins.2017.00702) — EXCLUDE.** APOE4 expression dysregulated the endosomal-lysosomal pathway in vivo, but evidence is transcriptomic plus endosome morphology only; no lysosomal function assay and no lipid endpoint.
9. **Cao J 2021 (10.1038/s41380-020-0824-3) — EXCLUDE.** The manipulated variable is miR-195; although it rescues ApoE4-associated lysosomal defects in iPSC-derived brain cells, the abstract reports no direct APOE4-vs-APOE3 test of lysosomal function or lipid handling in the named cell types.
10. **Miranda AM 2022 (10.1038/s41398-022-01881-6) — EXCLUDE.** APOE4 allelic dosage altered entorhinal-cortex lipidomes in targeted-replacement mice (including lysosome-associated BMP species), but this is tissue-level lipidomics without cell-resolved intracellular accumulation in astrocytes, microglia, or neurons. (Note: Screener A cited this paper as 10.1016/j.jlr.2023.100350, which is a different record in the pool — Cashikar AG et al. on 25-hydroxycholesterol regulation of astrocyte lipid metabolism; the correct DOI for the Miranda paper is 10.1038/s41398-022-01881-6.)
11. **Ritzel RM 2023 (10.1126/sciadv.add1101) — EXCLUDE.** Aged/injured microglia showed lysosomal burden and lipid accumulation, but APOE4 genotype was only a modifier of an age/brain-injury phenotype, not the manipulated or compared variable.
12. **Claes C 2021 (10.1186/s13024-021-00473-0) — EXCLUDE.** Plaque-associated human microglia accumulated lipid droplets in a chimeric AD model, but the manipulated variable was TREM2 R47H, not APOE4.
13. **Moulton MJ 2021 (10.1073/pnas.2112095118) — EXCLUDE.** ROS-induced neuron-to-glia lipid droplet formation in Drosophila depended on AD risk genes; a humanized APOE4 fly was used for an ABCA1-agonist rescue, but no APOE4-vs-APOE3 lipid accumulation comparison is reported in the abstract.
