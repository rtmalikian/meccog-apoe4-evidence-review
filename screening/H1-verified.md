# H1 Verification — Screener B (independent second pass)

**Hypothesis H1:** "In some types of non-Alzheimer's, non-aged human brain cells, for example astrocytes, APOE4 causes reduced endosome pH compared with APOE3."

**Verification criterion applied:** the paper is an experimental study that directly tests APOE4 vs APOE3/APOE2, or APOE4 manipulation, on endosomal/endosome pH or acidification in brain cells — i.e. it either (a) measures endosomal/lysosomal pH or acidification by APOE isoform, or (b) manipulates endosomal acidification within an APOE isoform comparison. Model systems (animal, cell-line, iPSC-derived) count. Excluded: reviews/meta-analyses, GWAS-only, clinical/epidemiological correlation-only work, editorials/protocols, and keyword-only matches (pH mentioned but never measured or manipulated; endosome biology with no pH readout and no acidification manipulation). Verification was made from the abstracts in `work/searches2/H1.json` (Run 1, 200 records) and `work/searches2/H1r2.json` (Run 2, 200 records) only; deduped union = 322 records, independently re-derived by Screener B (PMID, fallback DOI, then title).

**Starting-reference verdict (stated prominently):** Prasad H, Rao R, PNAS 2018, PMID 29946028, DOI 10.1073/pnas.1801612115, "Amyloid clearance defect in ApoE4 astrocytes is reversed by epigenetic correction of endosomal pH" — **VERIFIED ELIGIBLE and CONFIRMED, ranked #1.** It is present in both Run 1 and Run 2 with consistent PMID/DOI/title, and its abstract directly reports the H1 comparison: ApoE4 astrocytes, compared with the nonpathogenic ApoE3 allele, show aberrant/pathological endosome acidification (reduced endosomal pH) caused by ε4-selective down-regulation of the endosomal Na+/H+ exchanger NHE6, with HDAC inhibitors normalising the ApoE4-specific endosomal pH defect. No basis was found to flag it as ineligible.

## Confirmed

1. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1801612115 — 2018 — Prasad H — "Amyloid clearance defect in ApoE4 astrocytes is reversed by epigenetic correction of endosomal pH." (PMID 29946028. The only paper in the pool that directly measures an APOE4-vs-APOE3 endosomal pH difference in brain cells: ApoE4 astrocytes over-acidify endosomes via selective NHE6 down-regulation downstream of nuclear HDAC4; HDAC inhibition restored NHE6 and normalised endosomal pH, LRP1 trafficking and Aβ clearance; NHE6-knockout mice showed elevated brain Aβ. Direction supports H1. Present in both runs.)
2. DOI: 10.7554/elife.40048 — 2018 — Xian X — "Reversal of ApoE4-induced recycling block as a novel prevention approach for Alzheimer's disease." (PMID 30375977. Confirmed under the acidification-manipulation arm, with a caveat: the abstract reports no direct APOE4-vs-APOE3 endosomal pH measurement. It does, however, run an explicit ApoE4-vs-ApoE3 framework — ApoE4 differs from ApoE3 by a single amino acid raising its isoelectric point and promoting unfolding upon endosomal vesicle acidification — and it directly manipulates endosomal acidification by pharmacological and genetic inhibition of NHE6, the primary early-endosome proton-leak channel, completely reversing the ApoE4-induced recycling block of ApoER2/LRP8 and co-endocytosed AMPA/NMDA receptors and restoring Reelin modulation of excitatory synapses in rodents. Endosomal acidification is therefore the manipulated mechanistic variable in an isoform comparison, not a keyword mention. Note the directional tension with Prasad 2018: here further acidification (NHE6 inhibition) rescues, whereas Prasad attributes the ApoE4 defect to over-acidification from NHE6 loss; the paper is included as a direct experimental test of the APOE4–acidification link regardless of direction. Present in both runs.)

## Disputed

1. **DOI: 10.7554/elife.72034 — 2021 — Pohlkamp T — "NHE6 depletion corrects ApoE4-mediated synaptic impairments and reduces amyloid plaque load."** (PMID 34617884; present in Run 2 only.)
   Reason: held out as genuinely arguable both ways, so neither confirmed with Screener A nor excluded outright. For inclusion (A's position): like Xian 2018, it manipulates endosomal acidification — enhancing acidification by depleting/inhibiting NHE6 (Slc9a6) — and thereby corrects ApoE4-mediated endolysosomal trafficking and synaptic defects in mice, on the stated rationale that ApoE4's isoelectric point matches early-endosome pH and delays its dissociation from ApoE receptors. Against inclusion: endosome pH itself is never measured or compared by isoform — pH enters only as the manipulated rescue variable, with trafficking, synaptic homeostasis and plaque load as readouts — and, decisively, the authors report that NHE6 loss suppressed amyloid deposition *even in the absence of ApoE4* and frame NHE6 suppression as a "universal, ApoE-independent approach," so the study does not isolate an APOE4 (vs APOE3) effect on endosome pH/acidification. Whether a rescue-by-acidification study whose headline effect is ApoE-independent counts as "directly testing APOE4 on endosome pH" cannot be reconciled between the screeners on the abstract alone; it is therefore DISPUTED and held out of the confirmed list.

## Borderline-excluded

1. **DOI: 10.1186/s13024-020-00382-8 — 2020 — Larramona-Arcas R — "Sex-dependent calcium hyperactivity due to lysosomal-related dysfunction in astrocytes from APOE4 versus APOE3 gene targeted replacement mice."** (PMID 32517777.) Closest near-miss in the pool: a genuine APOE4-vs-APOE3 astrocyte comparison localising the defect to lysosomal-enriched *acidic stores* — but the measured variable is Ca2+ signalling/fluxes (plus lipidomics), not pH; endosomal/lysosomal pH is never measured and acidification is never manipulated. Excluded under the keyword-only / no-pH-readout rule; A's exclusion upheld after explicit adjudication.
2. **DOI: 10.1016/j.celrep.2020.108224 — 2020 — Narayan P — "PICALM Rescues Endocytic Defects Caused by the Alzheimer's Disease Risk Factor APOE4."** (PMID 33027662.) Direct APOE4-vs-isogenic-APOE3 endocytic comparison in human iPSC-derived astrocytes, but the readouts are endocytosis/growth and PICALM rescue; no pH measurement and no acidification manipulation. Excluded; A's exclusion upheld.
3. **DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2023.01.016 — 2023 — (LRP2 structural study) — "Structures of LRP2 reveal a molecular machine for endocytosis."** (PMID 36750096.) Cryo-EM structures of (kidney) LRP2 solved *at* endosomal pH — pH is an experimental condition, not an outcome, and there is no APOE isoform comparison. Keyword/condition-only match; excluded.
