# H1 Verification — Screener B (independent second pass)

**Hypothesis H1:** "APOE4 has reduced lipid transport activity, relative to APOE3, through the ABCA1 transporter, at the blood brain barrier and the blood cerebrospinal fluid barrier. This results in lipid depletion and stress on the barriers, and in less lipid being available for neurons and glia, resulting in reduced function of both cell types."

**Verification criterion applied:** the paper is an experimental study that directly tests APOE4 (vs APOE3/APOE2, or APOE4 manipulation) effects on ABCA1-mediated lipid transport / cholesterol efflux (apoE lipidation counted as the direct readout of ABCA1-mediated lipid transfer onto apoE in CNS), or on lipid availability at the blood-brain or blood-CSF barrier. Verification was made from the abstracts in `work/searches/H1.json` and `work/searches/H1r2.json` only.

## Disputed

1. **DOI: 10.1523/jneurosci.1937-12.2012 — 2012 — Fitz NF — "Abca1 deficiency affects Alzheimer's disease-like phenotype in human ApoE4 but not in ApoE3-targeted replacement mice."**
   Reason: the experiment manipulates Abca1 gene dose on human ApoE4 vs ApoE3 backgrounds, but per the abstract it measures no lipid-transport endpoint — no cholesterol efflux, no apoE lipidation; the readouts are amyloid deposition, cognition, Aβ clearance, and plasma HDL/Aβ42. It tests whether ABCA1 loss modifies the ApoE4 phenotype, not whether APOE4 reduces lipid transport through ABCA1.

2. **DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2112095118 — 2021 — Moulton MJ — "Neuronal ROS-induced glial lipid droplet formation is altered by loss of Alzheimer's disease-associated genes."**
   Reason: the study is a screen of AD risk-gene homologs (including ABCA1) for effects on ROS-induced neuron-to-glia lipid transfer in flies; per the abstract there is no APOE4-vs-APOE3/APOE2 comparison — APOE4 appears only as the background of a humanized fly model in which an ABCA1 agonist peptide restores lipid droplet formation. The APOE isoform effect on ABCA1-mediated transport is not directly tested.
